# RKN.STRUCT™ — Egypt B2B Outreach Campaign

Start here: **CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md** — strategy, phases, target segments, and
the pre-launch checklist.

Then use:
- **CONTENT_CALENDAR.md** — 4-week Instagram post plan, written in the
  company profile's design language (spaced caps, section numbers,
  SVC-XX/OBJ-XX codes).
- **DM_SCRIPTS.md** — Instagram DM openers, follow-ups, and reply
  handling, per vertical (restaurants, clothing brands, hotels, interior
  designers, furniture brands, artists).
- **EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md** — cold email templates for the same verticals,
  for contacts better reached by email than DM.
- **PROSPECT_TRACKER.csv** — outreach tracker template. Duplicate rows per
  prospect, fill in as you build the list and move contacts through the
  pipeline.

Source: `Company Profile` PDF (RKN.STRUCT B2B Capabilities Overview).
