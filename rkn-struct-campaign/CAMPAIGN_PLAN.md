# 01 / WHY THIS CAMPAIGN WORKS

RKN.STRUCT has three things most metal workshops in Egypt don't:

1. **A real design identity.** The Instagram grid and the profile deck read like a
   product studio, not a workshop — spaced-letter type, numbered systems
   (SVC-01, OBJ-01), document-style layouts. That alone puts RKN ahead of 90%
   of fabrication competitors, who sell on price because they have nothing
   else to sell on.
2. **A dual model.** Consumer 1-of-1 objects AND B2B manufacturing, same
   design language kept across both. Brands don't just get "a metal guy" —
   they get a studio that already thinks like a brand.
3. **A capital-free B2B engine.** No inventory, no upfront tooling spend.
   Deposit funds the build. This means RKN can say yes to almost any brief
   size without cash-flow risk — a real edge worth stating out loud to
   prospects, because it's *why* RKN can be flexible on custom work where
   bigger manufacturers say no.

The campaign below is built to sell those three things — not "we do metal
work" — to the six verticals in the profile: restaurants, clothing brands,
hotels, interior design studios, furniture brands, and independent artists.

# 02 / THE B2B LOOP (what you're actually selling)

This is the process to state plainly in every pitch — it's a trust
mechanism, not just logistics:

```
01 BRIEF        client shares idea/references         (1–2 days)
02 DESIGN       CAD + 3D render, client approves       (3–7 days)
   → DEPOSIT    client pays deposit, funds production
03 FABRICATE    laser cut / form / weld / finish       (in-house, Cairo)
   → BALANCE    remaining payment due on delivery
04 DELIVER      packaged, installed, aftercare
```

Say this to prospects explicitly: *"You see the exact 3D render before
anything is cut. You only pay the balance once it's in your hands."* That
single sentence removes the two biggest fears a brand has about hiring an
unfamiliar fabricator — surprise results, and paying upfront for nothing.

**Proof point to use everywhere:** RKN has already delivered a 200-piece
run of custom metal matchboxes for a wedding planner. Use this as the
answer to the objection every prospect is quietly thinking — *"can a
one-off object studio actually handle my quantity?"* Yes: 200 identical,
on-spec units, produced in-house. This is the single strongest case study
in the arsenal until more B2B projects are delivered — lead with it.

a real budget, and a planner actively sourcing "something no one else has"

more emotional close than most B2B categories, and it's recurring: one
happy planner books RKN again for their next 5–10 events a year, and tags
RKN in wedding content seen by thousands of prospective couples and other
planners.

# 04 / CAMPAIGN PHASES

**Phase 0 — Foundation (days 1–5)**
- Build the prospect list per segment (see PROSPECT_TRACKER.csv) — aim for
  40–60 qualified accounts before the first DM goes out.
- Shoot/collect real photo + video of finished pieces and the fabrication
  process (cutting, welding, finishing). This is the single highest-leverage
  thing to do before outreach starts — a brand will not wire a deposit to a
  page with placeholder photos. Even raw phone footage of sparks/welding
  outperforms stock imagery for this brand.
- Set up the tracker: Identified → Warmed Up → DM Sent → Replied →
  Call Booked → Proposal Sent → Deposit Paid → In Production → Delivered →
  Case Study Posted.

**Phase 1 — Content engine (ongoing, starts day 1, never stops)**
Instagram is the credibility layer that makes the cold DM/email land. See
CONTENT_CALENDAR.md for the 4-week plan. Cadence: 3–4 feed posts/reels per
week + daily stories.

**Phase 2 — Outreach (starts day 3, after 2–3 posts are live)**
DM + email in parallel. See DM_SCRIPTS.md and EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md.
Sequence per prospect:
1. Warm up — like/comment genuinely on 2–3 of their posts over 2–3 days.
2. Day 0 — send DM (or email if no active Instagram / more corporate,
   e.g. hotel groups, established furniture brands).
3. Day 3 — soft follow-up bump if no reply.
4. Day 7 — value-add follow-up: send one relevant portfolio piece or a
   quick mockup idea for their space, no pressure.
5. No reply after that — move to "Parked," recheck in 60 days.

**Phase 3 — Close & fulfill**
Use the B2B Loop language above verbatim in every proposal. Get to a paid
deposit as fast as possible — it's the real "yes."

**Phase 4 — Compound**
Every delivered project becomes content (Phase 1 pillar: "B2B Delivered").
Ask every satisfied client for a tagged repost and a short quote. This is
what turns a cold-outreach campaign into a referral engine after month 2–3.
