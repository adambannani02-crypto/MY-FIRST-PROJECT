# RULES FOR EVERY DM

1. **Warm up first.** Like/comment genuinely on 2–3 of their posts over
   2–3 days before sending anything. Cold DMs to zero-interaction accounts
   get ignored or reported.
2. **Reference something specific to them** in line 1 — their new location,
   a recent post, their aesthetic. Never open with "Hi, we are RKN.STRUCT,
   a creative studio that..." — that's a broadcast, not a message.
3. **One CTA per message.** Don't pitch the whole service list. Ask one
   small question that's easy to say yes to.
4. **Keep it under ~60 words.** These are read on a phone between other
   things.
5. Cadence per prospect: Day 0 (opener) → Day 3 (bump) → Day 7
   (value-add) → park if no reply.

# GENERAL REPLY HANDLING

**If they ask "how much does it cost?" before any brief exists:**
```
Depends entirely on size/material/finish — happy to give you a real number
once we know roughly what you're picturing. Can you share a photo/sketch
or a couple of reference images? Takes 5 min and I can give you a ballpark
same day.
```

**If they say "send more info":**
Don't paste the whole profile deck into the DM. Send the trimmed one-page
PDF (see CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md checklist) and one relevant portfolio photo, then
ask the qualifying question again.

**If they go quiet after showing interest:**
```
No pressure at all — whenever the timing's right, we're here. Saving your
idea on our end either way.
```
