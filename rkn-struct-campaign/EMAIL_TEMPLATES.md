# WHEN TO USE EMAIL OVER DM

Use email for accounts with a corporate structure or a generic inquiry
inbox where a DM won't reach a decision-maker: hotel groups/management
companies, established furniture brands, architecture firms, larger
interior design studios, procurement/purchasing contacts. Use Instagram DM
for independently-run restaurants, cafés, clothing brands, and individual
artists — that's where they actually respond.

Every email should attach the trimmed one-page PDF profile (see
CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md checklist) — never the full 12-page deck cold.
